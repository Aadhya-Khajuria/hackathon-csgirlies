Mental Wellbeing

Importance

Mental wellbeing is an important part of overall health. Looking after your mind can help you manage everyday stress, understand your emotions, build healthy habits, and enjoy the things that matter to you.

Why to Maintain?

✦

Manage everyday stress

✦

Understand your emotions

✦

Improve focus and concentration

✦

Build healthy relationships

Activities for mental wellness

Journaling

Write down your thoughts, feelings, or something that happened during your day.

Listen to Music

Put on a song or playlist that helps you relax or lifts your mood.

Meditation

15 minutes

Art challenge

Let your creativity flow without worrying about making it perfect.

Take a Walk

Step outside for a short walk and pay attention to your surroundings.

Talk to Someone

Even a simple conversation with a friend or a family member can help you feel connected.

Spend Time Outside

Sit somewhere peaceful, look around, or enjoy some fresh air.

Spend Time With a Pet

If you have a pet, spend some time playing with them, sitting with them, or taking them for a walk if appropriate.
