## Wellify
Wellify is designed to help you embody an active role in your lifestyle. Our goal is to help you make intentional choices that lead to a healthy and fulfilling life, whatever that may look like for you.

## Our Mission
Wellify is targeted toward anyone who want to improve their physical and mental well-being through daily habits, mood tracking, and self-care activities. Users who want an active role in their lifestyle and healthy choices and people looking for a private way to track wellness data.

## What does Wellify actually do?
We are excited to help create a better balance between physical and mental health care by encouraging users to practice mindfulness. This website provides a safe space where you can glow and grow and some of our features include:
-Education pages about physical and mental health
-Mood tracker
-Mindfulness tracker
-Health tracker
-Daily planner
-Self care activities (mental and physical)
-Kindness wall

## Why we built this
Our inspiration for this project was that there is a very important connection between your physical and mental health, and we need to preserve and cultivate that connection.

## How did you build Wellify?
We built Wellify using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, combining interactive wellness tools, mental and physical health resources, activity generators, and a simple design focused on the everyday wellbeing of the user.  

## What challenges did you run into while building Wellify?
One of the more prominent challenges we ran into was that we had to switch editor as it wasn’t working so we needed to use our quick intellectual thinking to get all our code up and running on the new editor.

## What accomplishments are you proud of?
An accomplishment we are proud of is the fact that we were able to switch to a new editor so quickly and effectively and we still had time left to code and add a Meet the Team page and footer last-minute.

## What we learned?
The overall takeaway from this experience is that we have improved our javascript skills and have even learnt how to automatically save things in our research!
